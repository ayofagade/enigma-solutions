How it works

Short engagements, working software.

Most projects run two to six weeks. You see something working in the first week, not a slide deck at the end of the month.

Step 01

Discovery

A call and a walkthrough of the process as it happens today. I map it, find where the time actually goes, and tell you if automation is the wrong answer.

Step 02

Scope

A written scope with the outcome, the integrations involved, what is out of scope and a fixed price. No open-ended hourly drift.

Step 03

Build

I build it, in your stack, against your real edge cases. You get a working version early and we iterate on the parts that matter.

Step 04

Handover

Documentation, a walkthrough with the people who use it, and monitoring so failures surface loudly. Support after go-live if you want it.
